### Application
Fining an orthogonal basis
$$
\text{given }\vec{x} = \begin{bmatrix}
1 \\
i
\end{bmatrix}\text{ find }\vec{y}\text{ such that }\{ \vec{x},\vec{y} \}\text{ is an orthogonal basis} \\
\\
\vec{y} = \begin{bmatrix}
a \\
b
\end{bmatrix}\\
\\
\left\langle \vec{x},\vec{y} \right\rangle = \overline{a} + i \cdot\overline{b} = 0 \\
\\
\text{by inspection: }a = 1, \ b = -i \text{ is a solution} \\
\\
\vec{y} = \begin{bmatrix}
1 \\
-i
\end{bmatrix}
$$
